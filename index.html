ASTRA is a guild built on fairness, respect, and teamwork.

We value clear communication, a cooperative attitude, and consistent participation as we help each other grow stronger.

Our Expectations

  • Communication — Stay updated on Discord and keep Management informed of your availability.
  • Respect — Treat guildmates respectfully, including during disagreements.
  • Attitude — Be honest, cooperative, and open to constructive feedback.
  • Fairness & Teamwork — Recognize effort and participation, share knowledge, and support each other’s growth.

Questions and constructive suggestions are welcome. Honest mistakes are understood as part of learning.
