Section 01

Who We Are

Our mission is to help each other become stronger through fairness, respect, and unity — while enjoying the game together.

We welcome players of all skill levels who share our values and are willing to contribute to the team.

01

Fairness

Rewards and opportunities are earned through effort, participation, and progression needs. The same rules apply to every member.

  • Be honest. No stealing, lying, or false excuses to obtain items.
  • Use guild support responsibly. Guild items are meant to strengthen your character. See Rewards & Distribution for details.
02

Respect

Treat others with courtesy, both inside the guild and in the wider community.

  • Communicate respectfully. Feedback and disagreements are welcome when handled calmly.
  • No toxicity. Disrespect, harassment, and aggression toward members will not be tolerated.
  • Represent the guild well. No arguing, baiting, or drama in world chat — even when provoked.
  • Keep guild matters private. Bring disputes to a Leader.
03

Unity

We rise together — one guild, one team.

Contribute where you can, cooperate during activities, and help fellow members grow.

04

Accountability

Honor your commitments and own your actions. Mistakes are part of learning; repeatedly ignoring the rules is not.

  • Stay active on Discord — announcements go there first.
  • Communicate your availability — tell Leaders early about absences.
  • Keep Leaders informed — IGN changes, character sales, status changes.
  • Use your profile notes — share updates with the guild (but still notify Leaders directly for important changes).
05

Fun

This is a game.

Enjoy it, make memories, and have fun — without taking that enjoyment away from others.

Section 03

Rewards & Distribution

Built on transparency, trust, and effort

Every reward in ASTRA is earned. There is nothing hidden about how we distribute what we collect. This is how it works:

  1. 1

    We defeat bosses

  2. 2

    We get loot

  3. 3

    We sell loot (inside or outside the guild)

  4. 4

    We collect the funds

  5. 5

    We distribute them back to members based on effort and participation.

The more you show up, the more you earn. It's that simple.

Check the website for Boss attendance to understand more about this. Check #distribution for past rewards.

Typical Reward Distribution (Field Boss Attendance)

To qualify for rewards in a given period, your attendance points must reach at least 30% of the Top 1 member's total points for that period.

Example: if the Top 1 member has 100 points, you need at least 30 to qualify.

High-Value Items, Boss Item Logs, and Collections

High-value items, Item/Boss Item Logs, and Collection pieces are allocated based on combat power (CP) and character need. Priority goes to the eligible member who will benefit most.

New Members

For new members, Probation lasts for 1–3 weeks.

Guild Auction

The in-game Guild Auction is open to everyone, including members on probation.

Where it happens on Discord

#distribution-rules

More details on how some of the items are handled can be read here.

#buy-request

Request items you need from Guild Storage — especially when multiple are available. Check previous posts for the required format before submitting.

#raffle

Leaders post items available for raffle, such as ability books and mounts. Vote only for items you want and can afford.

Winning gives you the opportunity to buy the item at a discounted price. Have enough diamonds ready before voting — don't blindly click.

If you win but cannot pay, you may lose the item, and your eligibility for future raffles may be affected.

#special-item-log & #special-ability-log

Post before-and-after screenshots for guild-provided items that are registered or consumed:

  • Item collections / collection logs
  • Mounts
  • Passive skills
  • Abilities and skill books
  • Any other item that uses the same system

How to log:

  1. Before using the item, screenshot the slot showing it has not been learned/registered.
  2. Use or register the item.
  3. Screenshot the result showing it has been learned/registered.
  4. Post both screenshots in the appropriate channel.

Example: The guild sells you a skill book at a discount. Screenshot the empty skill slot, learn the skill, then screenshot the learned skill.

Check existing posts in each channel for the expected format.

Important

Guild Support Items

Any item received from the guild — free, discounted, raffled, or from Guild Storage — is meant for the growth of your own character.

  • Do not resell guild support items without prior Leader approval.
  • If you want to replace, upgrade, or reinvest an item's value, discuss it with a Leader before selling.
  • Reselling guild support items for profit (e.g. buying at a discount and selling at market price) is a breach of trust and will affect your standing in the guild.
  • Items won through the in-game Guild Auction are yours and are not bound by all of the restrictions above — but they are still expected to be used for your character's growth, not personal profit.

Section 04

Field Boss Rules

Field bosses run on a shared rhythm. Know the rules before you join a rally.

Discord & Notifications

  1. Familiarize yourself with the guild Discord settings — especially the Boss Timer channel and bot.
  2. An automatic alarm sounds in the Voice Chat Lounge a couple of minutes before a boss spawns.

Party Leader Order

Party leaders go first in this order:

  1. Guild Leaders
  2. Officers
  3. Guild Supports
  4. Seniors
  5. Highest CP members

Check the ASTRA website roster to see who holds each role and any recent changes.

Rally & Timing

  1. Arrive at the rally point 1–2 minutes before the boss respawns.
  2. Some bosses spawn far away — factor in travel time, especially without a Teleport Stone.
  3. A rally screenshot is taken when the boss spawns. This is used for attendance verification.
  4. A loot screenshot is taken after the boss is defeated.
  5. Failing to join the rally before the boss spawns is considered late.

Late Arrivals

Leaders will make an effort to accommodate latecomers, such as retaking the rally screenshot, but this is not guaranteed. Late participation earns attendance points only if a retaken screenshot includes you. Repeated lateness may affect future considerations.

Participation

  1. Only join the rally if you intend to participate in the battle.

Open Bosses

Venatus, Viorent, and Ego are open bosses. Anyone may participate without a strict rally. Stay updated through Discord announcements, as the list of open bosses may change.

Loot & Storage

  1. The designated leader is responsible for looting boss drops and storing them in Guild Storage after the boss (or boss set) is defeated.
  2. All items must be stored in Guild Storage. Check #boss-rules for updated exceptions.
  3. The leader may keep items that are exempted.

Team Cooperation

Sharing responsibilities — loot storage, timer management, screenshot capture — keeps field bosses running smoothly. Let the team know if you can help cover the timer or screenshots.

Attendance & Corrections

Rally and loot screenshots are posted in #boss-logs. This is the attendance record.

  1. Double-check your attendance in #boss-logs after each boss.
  2. Always keep a backup screenshot as proof, especially if you were not in the party.
  3. To request a correction, post your backup screenshot in #boss-logs and tag a Leader.
  4. Attendance points are awarded based on the number of bosses attended.

Code of Conduct

We tackle bosses as a team. Following these rules is a courtesy to Management and to every other member. Help create efficient and enjoyable boss battles for everyone.
